## About Duhang Middle School

Duhang Middle School is a well-known **“starter quest”** among local urban exploration enthusiasts. Abandoned in 2008, it has now been 18 years since its closure (as of this writing).

Honestly, I don't consider myself an “urban exploration enthusiast.” After all, exploring abandoned buildings often means maneuvering through **dusty**, **dirty**, and **messy** environments, frequently leaving you covered in grime. If you're unlucky, you might get **injured** by unstable structures or have to confront patrolling **security guards** or **police**.

But I enjoy capturing **“traces left by time”**, or more accurately, **_“artificial structures transformed by nature”_**.

When I first rode my motorcycle to Duhang Middle School, I felt nothing special. But on my second visit, I noticed some objects and structures had changed since my previous trip, and several workers seemed to be conducting construction on-site.

It suddenly dawned on me: **everything here that seemed abandoned and timeless might soon undergo irreversible change**. This reminded me of a friend’s words: **“Some seemingly ordinary photos may only be captured once in this world.”**

Thus, whether driven by kitsch or the need for “emergency preservation photography”, I returned multiple times in quick succession with my `Pentax 67II` and `Technikardan S45`. The `Technikardan` handled perspective and spatial composition, while the `Pentax 67II` captured close-ups and exploited perspective distortion to reveal unique beauty.
